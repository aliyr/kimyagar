# چهره‌های مشتریان پیشخوان

این برگه تنها منبع حقیقت برای پرترهٔ مشتریان پیشخوان است و هیچ تصویری بدون یک ردیف در همین فایل ساخته نمی‌شود.
هر ردیف یک انسان جداست؛ چهره، سن، نشان چهره و ترکیب رنگ و مدل مو باید از ردیف‌های دیگر متمایز باشد.
پوشاک کلاسیک دوران صفوی و قاجار است؛ چهره‌ها ایرانیِ امروزند و تنوع واقعی اقوام ایران را نشان می‌دهند.
جملهٔ سبک پایین برای همهٔ تصویرها یکسان است و بی‌تغییر، همراه هر ردیف، به کار می‌رود.

## سبک مشترک

Game character asset, single isolated character on flat #d8d2c8 background, seen from the waist up facing slightly left, painterly cozy Persian fantasy game-art style, warm lighting, 3:4 portrait.

Young women are modern-day Iranian faces: very beautiful and alluring, glamorous daf look, with striking eyes, styled brows, full lips, and a confident or flirty expression. Coverage stays modest. Every young woman wears a Qajar or Safavid costume with a HIGH CLOSED NECKLINE, chest fully covered, no cleavage, no off-shoulder. She wears a loose decorative headscarf (fake hijab): the scarf sits far back or is slipping, lots of hair visible, neck visible above the high collar. The bride keeps a lace scarf. The dancer keeps a coin-edged scarf, and her costume still has a high collar.

Young men are handsome and athletic. The sport shows in the period cut of the clothes and in one prop. No modern sportswear and no bare torso. Forearms may show, and a sleeveless period vest is allowed over a covered chest.

Older women may wear a full traditional head covering. They are dignified and warm, with no glamour makeup. Chest covered.

## قواعد حالت

These three poses share one face, one wardrobe, and one framing. Only the hands and the expression change. Face identity must not change between the three.

- IDLE = standing behind the counter, hands low near the waist, and she or he may hold the trade prop low at the waist.
- HAPPY = the same person, the same clothes, the same framing, one hand reaching forward-down and holding a small corked glass potion bottle with glowing liquid, delighted smile, bright eyes.
- SAD = the same person, the same clothes, the same framing, holding the same potion bottle, disappointed: closed mouth, lowered brows, slight frown.

The English paragraph under each table is the identity prompt (age, face, hair, wardrobe, prop). Apply the pose rules on top of it.
